# Short Circuit — קצר — game identity

## One line

Gunpoint × Monaco in 3D: a night heist through the Electric Museum where every camera, laser, alarm loop, motor door and corridor light is a load on a real circuit hidden in the walls. Circuits are the lockpick.

## Core action without the school topic

Sneak through a guarded building in real time, toggle an X-ray view that reveals the building's hidden systems, and rewire wall panels so the building's own machines open your path — then live with the consequences (dark rooms make guards curious, a popped fuse sends a guard to the fuse box, a broken alarm loop screams).

## 60-second play sequence (heist 1)

1. Night, museum lobby. The thief (hoodie, headlamp, backpack) crouches by the entrance. A guard sits at a desk across a brightly lit lobby with a visible vision cone; a ceiling camera sweeps the side corridor.
2. Hold the scanner: walls turn to dark glass, wires glow neon cyan inside them, little current dots stream from a wall panel to the lobby chandelier and to the camera.
3. Sneak to the panel (E). Camera swoops to a 3D close-up of the panel board: battery, brass terminal posts, glowing wires, the lamp and camera drawn as icons with live amp readings.
4. Click the lamp's wire → snip → the dots stop, the gauge reads 0 A, the lobby goes dark behind the panel. Back in the world the guard says "?" and gets up to check the panel — you have a few seconds to cross in the dark.
5. The exhibit room door has a gap in its circuit. In your bag: a rubber glove and a coin. The glove does nothing (insulator). The coin closes the circuit, the motor door grinds open. Grab the exhibit, run to the exit.

## Camera

Isometric 3D cut-away: orthographic camera at ~35° elevation, 45° yaw, rooms without ceilings, walls between the camera and the thief fade. Rotate in 90° steps (Q / E). Smooth follow. Panel close-up is a perspective 3D shot of the panel board.

## Visual thesis

A midnight science museum. Deep indigo/navy shadows and moonlight through tall windows; warm amber exhibit spotlights in pools on polished dark floors; neon-cyan wiring and current that pops against the dark; red for alarms, lasers and guard alert; soft yellow guard flashlights. Exhibits: a Tesla coil, a giant glass light bulb, Volta pile, old generators. Not toy-industrial, not paper/ink, not cartoon pastel. Think Monaco's readable top-down silhouettes + a modern stylized PBR look.

- Thief: slim teen-adult in a dark charcoal hoodie, cyan-trimmed sneakers, small headlamp, backpack with a wiring scanner. Readable silhouette from above.
- Guard: chunky museum night guard, navy uniform, cap, flashlight, round belly — comic, not menacing.
- Panels: grey-green steel wall panels with a hazard stripe and a lightning label.

## HUD (Hebrew RTL)

- Top right: heist name + objective ("גנבו את הנורה הענקית וצאו").
- Top left: alert meter (eye icon fills yellow → red), timer, ghost bonus star.
- Bottom center: scanner button (סורק), bag of items with icons, interact prompt.
- Panel close-up: board centered; right side shows the selected component (name, R, live V and I); bottom tray = bag items to place; buttons: חתוך (cut), סגור (close), מד-זרם toggle.
- Font: Rubik (bundled). Numbers/units in LTR spans: `2.4 A`, `12 V`, `5 Ω`.

## Feel

Snappy movement, crouch-sneak, guard "?" and "!" barks, a comic "busted" moment (guard flashlight in your face, thief freezes with hands up, sad trombone), fuse pop with sparks and smoke, alarm siren with red strobing, satisfying snips and clicks on the panel, current dots that visibly speed up and slow down.

## What it is not

Not a quiz with a door. Not a courier game. Not a circuit sandbox with a heist skin: every circuit change has a spatial stealth consequence and every stealth problem is solved through the circuit.
